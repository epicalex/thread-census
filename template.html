What counts as a thread? +

A moment where Lenny explicitly says he wants to pull or follow a conversational thread. One conversational move counts once, even if he mentions “a couple threads.” Guest remarks, ads, unrelated uses of “thread,” and immediately repeated interruptions are excluded.
